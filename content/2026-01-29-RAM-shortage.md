# The Global RAM Crisis: Why Memory Prices Are Exploding

*Published: January 2026*  
*Category: Technology / Economics*

---

## Introduction

A global shortage of computer memory — commonly called the **RAM crisis** or **“RAMmageddon”** — is currently affecting the technology industry. Prices for **DRAM and NAND memory chips have surged**, and shortages are impacting everything from laptops and smartphones to AI data centres. 

This crisis is not simply a supply chain problem. Instead, it is largely driven by a **massive surge in demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure**, which consumes enormous amounts of memory.

---

## What is RAM and Why It Matters

RAM (Random Access Memory) is one of the most important components in modern computing.

It allows computers to:

- Run applications quickly  
- Store active data temporarily  
- Process large workloads efficiently  

Without enough RAM, computers become slow or unable to handle complex tasks.

---

## The Main Cause: The AI Boom

The biggest driver of the current memory shortage is **artificial intelligence**.

Modern AI systems require vast amounts of memory for training and running models. Major technology companies are building enormous data centres filled with GPUs and servers that require **far more RAM than traditional computing systems**. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

As a result:

- AI data centres now consume a **huge share of global memory production**
- Manufacturers are prioritising **high-bandwidth memory (HBM)** used in AI servers
- Less conventional RAM is available for consumer devices

Some estimates suggest that **AI data centres consumed nearly 70% of global memory chips in 2025**, leaving shortages for other industries.

---

## Rapid Price Increases

The result has been a dramatic increase in prices.

Recent market data shows:

- DRAM prices rising **80–95% within a single quarter**
- NAND flash prices increasing **55–60%**
- Some RAM kits doubling in price in months 

In some cases, memory prices are fluctuating **hour by hour** as companies compete to secure supply. 

---

## Why Supply Cannot Catch Up Quickly

Memory manufacturing is extremely complex.

Only a few companies dominate the market:

- Samsung
- SK Hynix
- Micron

Building new semiconductor fabrication plants takes **years and billions of dollars**, so supply cannot increase rapidly.

At the same time, manufacturers are shifting production toward **higher-margin AI memory technologies**, further reducing the availability of consumer RAM. 

---

## Impact on Consumers

The RAM crisis is already affecting everyday technology.

Potential consequences include:

- Higher prices for laptops and desktops  
- Smartphones shipping with less memory  
- Delays in upgrading hardware  
- Rising costs for cloud computing services  

Industry analysts warn that memory shortages may persist **until at least 2027**. 
---

## A Structural Shift in Computing

Unlike previous semiconductor shortages, this crisis reflects a deeper transformation in the technology landscape.

Artificial intelligence is changing the economics of computing:

- AI systems require **massive datasets**
- GPUs need **huge memory bandwidth**
- Data centres are expanding rapidly

As long as the AI boom continues, memory will remain one of the most valuable and constrained resources in computing.

---

## Conclusion

The global RAM crisis highlights how quickly technological revolutions can reshape supply chains.

What was once a cheap commodity component has become a strategic resource. As AI infrastructure continues to expand, the competition for memory will likely intensify, affecting industries far beyond traditional computing.

The age of artificial intelligence may ultimately be remembered as the moment when **memory became the most valuable resource in computing**.
